LexiDraftFeedback & revision

Strengthen legal writing without outsourcing the thinking

LexiDraft acts as a high-level editor for students' existing writing. It can identify weaknesses in legal reasoning, clarity, structure and use of authority while keeping the student's analysis and final writing at the centre of the task.

  • Evaluate whether writing answers the question clearly and legally.
  • Identify logical gaps, vague analysis and weak application of doctrine.
  • Review structure, academic tone, legal authority and referencing.
  • Support revision of essays, case notes, memos and problem-question responses.

ChatGPT 5.4Teaching workflow

A flexible workspace for everyday legal education tasks

Use the agent for rapid drafting and iteration where the task is low-risk and the output will be reviewed by an academic or professional staff member.

  • Draft and refine assessment rubrics and marking guides.
  • Create realistic legal hypotheticals for tutorials and problem questions.
  • Generate formative questions, explanations and instructional material.
  • Transform quiz content into a Canvas-ready text2qti workflow.

Unit Outline HelperAKARI & alignment

Move from curriculum material to clearer unit-outline documentation

Unit Outline Helper supports planning and review of unit learning outcomes and assessment summaries for Sydney Curriculum (AKARI), with additional curriculum-mapping functions.

  • Draft or revise Unit Learning Outcomes (ULOs).
  • Draft assessment summaries for unit outlines.
  • Extract relevant curriculum information from existing text.
  • Review ULOs against Course Learning Outcomes, Sydney Graduate Qualities, AQF expectations and CALD Threshold Learning Outcomes.
  • Map assessments to ULOs using a 0–2 alignment scale and identify gaps or over-concentration.

Quick alignment prompt

After drafting ULOs and an assessment summary, ask:

Create a matrix showing the alignment between ULOs and assessments in this unit.

The supplied methodology uses a 0–2 scale: 0 = not addressed, 1 = partially addressed, and 2 = addressed.

Current configuration note: the supplied Unit Outline Helper guide identified an earlier ChatGPT4o configuration. Because the current underlying model is not identified in the supplied materials, this site does not hard-code a replacement model name.
Human review remains essential: confirm generated ULOs, assessment summaries and mapping judgements against current University curriculum requirements and the actual design of the unit.
