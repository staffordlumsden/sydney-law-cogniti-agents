## **Access the ChatGPT4o Agent**

1. Go to: [ChatGPT4o For SLS](https://app.cogniti.ai/agents/67ff16c50feb13ccd1bf9b1b/chat?k=mj4Gac-BTNmFxSWjhh1blQuio2n4kp9XAIjL3RYNus8)
2. If prompted, select **Login with Microsoft**.
3. Use your **UniKey**, **password**, and **Okta Verify** to log in.
4. No installation or technical setup is required.

## :cogniti_logo_circles_icon: **Meet “Cogniti”**

Cogniti is the platform hosting your AI agent.

You’ll be chatting with a **ChatGPT4o** model—fast, focused, and low-risk.

Think of it as a smart assistant that can help with things like:

- Drafting rubrics, marking guides, or scaffolded instructions
- Generating tutorial questions or legal hypotheticals
- Suggesting plain-English definitions or explanations
- Helping structure feedback or policy summaries

## **🛠 Try a Prompt**

You don’t need to be technical—just type what you need!

**Example prompts:**

- *“Create a 4-criteria rubric for a 3,000-word legal essay on contract law for second-year undergraduates.”*
- *“Give me three plain-English definitions of estoppel suitable for teaching first-year law students.”*
- *“Draft five tutorial questions based on a hypothetical about discrimination in the workplace.”*

## **✅ Tips**

- Keep prompts short but specific.
- Always review and edit AI outputs before sharing with students.
- Use it as a **first draft**, not a final product.
- ChatGPT4o for SLS is *text-only*—ideal for quick teaching and admin support.
