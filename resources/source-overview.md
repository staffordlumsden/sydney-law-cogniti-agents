# Sydney Law School–Developed Cogniti Agents

Sydney Law School has developed a growing suite of Cogniti agents to support students, academics and professional staff with common teaching, learning and administrative tasks. Each agent has been designed for a particular purpose, with guidance and safeguards appropriate to its intended use.

---

## [LexiDraft](https://app.cogniti.ai/agents/67ea5935cec5649fd916a975/chat?k=xaDzzehcImnvusiIEbp7471rEKbPe32mq2mkxN1_NAk)

**Strengthen your legal writing without outsourcing the thinking.**

LexiDraft is a specialist writing assistant for advanced law students, particularly those completing upper-level coursework or an Honours thesis. It provides targeted feedback on the **structure, clarity, legal reasoning and academic style** of your writing while keeping you firmly in control of the work.

Use LexiDraft to:

- test the clarity and organisation of an argument;
- strengthen legal reasoning;
- refine case notes, essays and problem-question responses;
- improve academic tone and expression; and
- review citations and referencing.

LexiDraft is designed as a **feedback and revision tool rather than a content-generation service**. Think of it as an additional critical reader: it can help identify where an argument could be clearer or better supported, but the analysis and final writing remain yours.

**Version:** v1.1_09052025 [Experimental]

> ⚠️ **Data notice:** This model is not deployed in an Australian geographic region. Do not enter personal, protected or highly protected data.

[View the LexiDraft Quick Start and help resources](https://canvas.sydney.edu.au/courses/11474/pages/welcome-to-lexidraft?module_item_id=2681568).

## [ChatGPT 5.4 for Sydney Law School](https://app.cogniti.ai/agents/67ff16c50feb13ccd1bf9b1b/chat?k=mj4Gac-BTNmFxSWjhh1blQuio2n4kp9XAIjL3RYNus8)

**A general-purpose AI workspace for everyday legal education tasks.**

ChatGPT 5.4 for Sydney Law School is a Cogniti agent configured for fast, low-risk support with common teaching and curriculum-development tasks. It is particularly useful where rapid drafting and iteration can streamline routine work without adding unnecessary complexity.

You might use it to:

- draft or refine assessment rubrics;
- develop hypothetical legal problems and scenarios;
- generate formative questions and activities;
- restructure or reformat instructional content;
- prepare quiz material for Canvas; or
- explore alternative ways of explaining a legal concept.

The agent is deliberately general purpose: you provide the context, constraints and source material, then apply professional judgement to review, verify and refine its output.

**Version:** v2_21052025

### Important limitations

- It is **not a search engine** and does not independently search the internet.
- Outputs should be checked against authoritative legal, policy and scholarly sources before use.
- The model is not hosted in Australia.
- Do not enter personal, protected or highly protected data.

## [Unit Outline Helper](https://app.cogniti.ai/agents/67f70e091c9e6dbda3bff188/chat?k=mOeUxp0d-QwaXfagYEyuqWAgu6ZqF4NJyDWMoFuuBV8)

**Turn curriculum ideas and existing material into clearer unit-outline content.**

Unit Outline Helper is a Cogniti agent developed for academic and professional staff working on **unit planning and Sydney Curriculum (AKARI) documentation**.

It can help you:

1. draft new **Unit Learning Outcomes (ULOs)**;
2. develop an **assessment summary** suitable for a unit outline;
3. develop both outcomes and assessment information together; or
4. extract relevant information from existing curriculum documents and reorganise it into the required format.

The agent is particularly useful at the early drafting and review stages, helping staff move from existing curriculum material or initial ideas towards clearer, more consistent unit-outline documentation. Its outputs should still be reviewed by the relevant academic and checked against University curriculum requirements before submission.

**Version:** v1.2_29042025

> ⚠️ **Data notice:** This model is not deployed in an Australian geographic region. Do not enter personal, protected or highly protected data.

[Access the Law Unit Outline Helper documents and Quick Start Guide](https://tinyurl.com/29uzspsd).

# AI Student Support Chatbot

**A first point of reference when a student may need non-academic support.**

The **AI Student Support Chatbot** helps staff identify appropriate information and possible next steps when supporting students who may be at risk or require non-academic assistance.

The chatbot draws on the University’s **[Red Book – Specialist Advice and Support for Students 2024](https://intranet.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/intranet/documents/teaching-support/teaching-resources/red-book-specialist-advice-and-support-for-students-2024-final.pdf)** and is intended to make relevant guidance easier to locate and navigate.

Staff can use it to explore questions such as:

- What University support might be appropriate for this student?
- Where should I refer a student for specialist assistance?
- What should I do next if I am concerned about a student?
- Which University service is responsible for a particular type of support?

The chatbot is a **navigation and information tool**. It does not replace professional judgement, specialist advice or emergency support.

[Access the AI Student Support Chatbot](https://bit.ly/AI_Student_Support) or use the accompanying QR code.

> ⚠️ **Important:** The chatbot does not provide medical, psychological or diagnostic advice and should not be relied upon as a substitute for a qualified health or wellbeing practitioner. As with any generative AI system, responses may occasionally be incomplete or inaccurate; staff should verify information where necessary and use established University referral and emergency procedures where appropriate.

For questions about student wellbeing support, contact [student.wellbeing@sydney.edu.au](mailto:student.wellbeing@sydney.edu.au).
