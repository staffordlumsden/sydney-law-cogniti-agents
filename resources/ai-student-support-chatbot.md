# AI Student Support Chatbot

**Audience:** Sydney Law School academic and professional staff  
**Purpose:** navigation to non-academic student support information

The AI Student Support Chatbot is designed to provide general information for staff about possible next actions when they identify a student who may be at risk or require non-academic support.

The supplied description states that the chatbot was generated using the University's *Red Book – Specialist Advice and Support for Students 2024* as its source material.

## What staff can use it for

The chatbot can help staff explore questions such as:

- What type of University support may be appropriate for this student?
- What kind of specialist service may be relevant?
- What might be an appropriate next step if I am concerned about a student?
- Which area of the University is responsible for a particular type of non-academic support?

## Appropriate role of the chatbot

The chatbot is an **information and navigation tool**. It can support staff in locating or interpreting general support information, but it does not replace:

- professional judgement;
- specialist health or wellbeing advice;
- emergency support;
- formal University procedures; or
- direct consultation with the relevant specialist service.

## Important disclaimer

The chatbot is not intended to provide medical, psychological or diagnostic advice and should not be relied upon as a substitute for a qualified health or wellbeing practitioner.

As with any generative AI system, responses may be incomplete or inaccurate. Staff should verify important information and use established University referral and emergency procedures where appropriate.
