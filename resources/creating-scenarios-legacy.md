# Creating Scenarios with ChatGPT4o

Hypothetical scenarios are the cornerstone of legal problem-solving tasks. This guide helps legal educators craft a clear, effective prompt to use with **ChatGPT4o for SLS** to generate appropriate scenarios for problem questions.

## **🔹 Step 1: State the Purpose**

Let the AI know that you’re creating a scenario for a legal problem question. This frames the response and helps the model avoid irrelevant detail.

**Example**:

```
I am a legal educator creating a hypothetical scenario for a law school problem question.
```

## **🔹 Step 2: Specify the Topic or Area of Law**

Be clear about the legal domain. This ensures the scenario stays relevant to the intended teaching outcome.

**Prompt Template**:

```
The scenario should relate to [insert topic, e.g., tort law—specifically nuisance and trespass].
```

## **🔹 Step 3: Set the Assessment Context**

Explain what the scenario will be used for (e.g., in-class activity, exam, online quiz), and if students are expected to apply IRAC or another problem-solving method.

**Example**:

```
It will be used in an in-class activity to help students apply IRAC and identify relevant legal issues.
```

## **🔹 Step 4: Indicate the Desired Complexity**

Mention how many legal issues or parties should be involved. This controls the scenario length and depth.

**Example**:

```
The scenario should include at least two identifiable legal issues and involve at least two parties.
```

## **🔹 Step 5: Keep it Realistic and Focused**

Let o3-mini know to avoid unrealistic or overly dramatic situations. Ask for scenarios that mirror typical legal fact patterns.

**Example**:

```
Keep the facts realistic and suitable for educational purposes, avoiding extreme or sensationalised events.
```

## **🔹 Step 6: (Optional) Follow-Up Questions**

If desired, ask o3-mini to generate follow-up questions or prompts that students can respond to after reading the hypothetical.

**Example**:

```
After the scenario, include 1–2 questions asking students to identify legal issues or apply legal principles.
```

## **🧩 Final Prompt Template**

Here’s what it looks like when combined:

```
I am a legal educator creating a hypothetical scenario for a law school problem question. 
- The scenario should relate to [insert area of law]. 
- It will be used in a [tutorial/class/exam] to help students apply IRAC and identify relevant legal issues. 

The scenario should:
1. include at least [number] legal issues.
2. involve at least [number] parties. 
3. Keep the facts realistic and suitable for educational purposes. 

After the scenario, include 1–2 questions asking students to identify or apply legal principles.
```

## 🎓 Differentiating between Undergraduate vs Postgraduate

**Undergraduate Scenario**

- **Issue Complexity:** 1–2 legal issues, straightforward facts
- **Context:** Local, easily relatable scenarios (e.g., neighbours, leases)
- **Language Level:** Clear, plain English
- **Guidance in Questions:** Specific prompts (e.g., “What torts are involved?”)
- **Assessment Focus:** Issue-spotting, basic application of principles

**Postgraduate Scenario**

- **Issue Complexity:** 3+ issues, often interrelated or ambiguous
- **Context:** Professionally framed (e.g., memos, client consultations)
- **Language Level:** Professional tone, nuanced terminology
- **Guidance in Questions:** Open-ended (e.g., “Advise the parties of their legal options”)
- **Assessment Focus:** Advanced reasoning, policy consideration, critique of doctrine

**✅ Tip**: Mention the level of students in your prompt.

```
This is for undergraduate/postgraduate students in [unit name].
```
